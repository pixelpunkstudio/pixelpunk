About this book

Big letters. Merry puzzles. Easy on the eyes.

Tired of squinting at tiny puzzle books? This jumbo Christmas word search for seniors prints every puzzle in extra large 30 font letters (big print, 30-point type), on clean white pages with no busy background, so you can relax and enjoy the season one festive theme at a time.

What's inside
  • 100 Christmas word searches in extra large print (30 font size), one per page on big 8.5 x 11 inch pages
  • Single sided: every puzzle sits on its own page, so nothing shows through and you can tear pages out
  • 18 words per puzzle in three easy columns, all printed as big as the grid letters
  • A fun fact beside every puzzle, from history and science to gardening, music, art and literature, plus space for notes
  • 1,800 words and not one repeats anywhere in the book
  • Large, clear solutions at the back, two per page
100 festive themes seniors love

Bake your way through a Christmas Cookie Swap, Grandma's Recipe Box and Homemade Fudge and Brittle. Step outside with Cardinals in the Snow, Holly and Ivy, Winter Berries and Woodland Animals in Winter. Remember A 1950s Christmas, Old-Time Radio Christmas and Vintage Christmas Decorations. Travel to Christmas in Ireland, Norway, Austria, Hawaii, Scotland, Louisiana and the Southwest, then enjoy The Christmas Concert, Classic Christmas Stories, Stained Glass Windows, The Christmas Night Sky and many more.

Made for comfortable, happy puzzling

The grids use big, well-spaced letters, and most words read forwards, which keeps every puzzle relaxing rather than frustrating. A simple how-to-play page helps beginners start right away, and every puzzle tells you exactly where to find its solution.

Perfect for
  • Seniors and older adults who want an easy, jumbo print word search book that doesn't strain the eyes

  • Nostalgic puzzle lovers who enjoy memories of a 1950s Christmas, old-time radio and vintage decorations
  • Families, caregivers and activity coordinators looking for calm brain games and memory care activities for the holidays
  • Anyone who loves Christmas and wants a cozy, screen-free activity book for adults
