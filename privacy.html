Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Pixel Punk Studio publishes puzzle and activity books (sold on Amazon) and runs the website pixelpunkstudio.shop. We collect as little personal information as possible. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

Pixel Punk Studio, Rue de Bourg 27, 1003 Lausanne, Switzerland.
Contact: hello@pixelpunkstudio.shop

Visiting our website

You don't need to give us any information to browse our website. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which, like any web host, may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs to deliver and secure the site. We do not have access to these logs.

Website statistics and cookies (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) to understand how visitors use our website: which pages are read, which free printables are popular, how people find us and roughly where they come from (country or city). This helps us decide which puzzles and articles to make next.

  • Google Analytics receives information such as the pages you visit, your browser and device type, the website that sent you, and an approximate location. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: a banner asks for your choice. Until you click "Accept", Google Analytics runs without cookies for you (Google's "consent mode" with analytics storage turned off), so no analytics cookie is placed on your device. If you accept, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies (named _ga) to tell new visits from returning ones.
  • Visitors elsewhere: Google Analytics uses first-party cookies (named _ga) to tell new visits from returning ones; you can turn them off with "Reject" in the cookie settings.
  • Your choice: we remember your choice in your browser's local storage (an entry named pps_consent, no cookie) for 180 days, then ask again. You can change it at any time with Cookie settings (also at the bottom of every page); choosing "Reject" deletes the Google Analytics cookies on this website.
  • We do not use advertising features, ad personalization or remarketing, and we never combine these statistics with your email address.
  • Google may process this data in the United States. You can block Google Analytics with your browser's privacy settings, a content blocker, or Google's opt-out browser add-on. More information: Google's privacy policy.

Free printable puzzles

You can download our free printables without giving any personal information.

Our newsletter

If you subscribe, we collect only your email address, to send you new releases, free puzzles and offers (at most one email a month) and a welcome email with a free puzzle pack.

  • Subscriptions are managed by our email provider Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which stores your address on our behalf in the European Union.
  • You confirm your subscription by clicking a link in a confirmation email (double opt-in).
  • Every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link. When you unsubscribe, you no longer receive our emails and you can ask us to delete your address completely.
  • We use standard email statistics (for example whether an email was opened or a link clicked) only to improve our newsletter.
  • We never sell, rent or share your email address.

Emails you send us

If you write to us, we use your message and email address only to reply. Our mailbox is hosted by Infomaniak (Switzerland).

Links to Amazon and social networks

Our book links lead to Amazon, and our posts appear on platforms such as Pinterest, Instagram and TikTok. When you visit them, their own privacy policies apply. We do not receive personal data about who clicks our links or buys our books.

Children

Some of our books are made for children, but this website and our newsletter are meant for adults (parents, grandparents, teachers). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has subscribed, contact us and we will delete the address.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, the European GDPR or US state privacy laws), you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use. Simply email hello@pixelpunkstudio.shop. You can also contact your local data protection authority.

How long we keep data

We keep your email address while you are subscribed. After you unsubscribe, it is kept only as an "unsubscribed" record so that we never email you again, unless you ask us to delete it.

Our social media publishing tool

To publish our own posts, we use a private internal tool ("Pixel Punk Publisher") connected to Pixel Punk Studio's own Pinterest, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it. The platforms give the tool access tokens and basic information about our own accounts (account ID, username, list of our boards), which it uses only to publish our posts and check that they were published. The tool does not collect, read or store any data about other users, followers or visitors. Tokens are stored only on our own computer and are never shared. Images posted to Instagram are temporarily hosted on this website because Instagram requires a public image address. We can revoke the tool's access at any time from each platform's settings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
