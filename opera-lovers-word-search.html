About this book

Great operas. Fascinating composers. One relaxing puzzle at a time.

This classical music word search book for adults takes you through three centuries of opera, from the Baroque age to the 1920s. Every two-page spread brings you a portrait of a composer, a guide to one of their great operas with its premiere, librettist and a famous aria, the composer's story and a verified fun fact, facing a large print word search of 30 words about that composer and their music.

What's inside
  • 55 composers and 55 operas, from the first great operas of the Baroque age to the bold new sounds of the 1920s
  • 55 large print word searches on big 8.5 x 11 inch pages, 20 x 20 grids with 30 words each
  • Single sided: every puzzle sits on its own right-hand page, so nothing shows through
  • A music page beside every puzzle: the composer's portrait or photograph in black and white, the opera's premiere, librettist and a famous aria, the composer's story and a surprising anecdote
  • 1,650 words and not one repeats anywhere in the book
  • Full solutions at the back, one per page
Meet the composers

Find out how Lully's own baton led to his death, why Rossini had a true birthday only once every four years, which conservatory turned down the teenage Verdi and now bears his name, where Dido and Aeneas had its first known performance, and why Bizet died thinking Carmen had failed. Meet Monteverdi, Handel, Mozart, Beethoven, Verdi, Wagner, Tchaikovsky, Puccini, Dvořák, Scott Joplin, Ethel Smyth and many more, including Purcell, Vivaldi, Gluck, Bellini, Donizetti, Gounod, Offenbach, Massenet, Rimsky-Korsakov, Debussy, Janáček and Ravel.

Easy on the eyes, rich in music

Big letters, clear grids and plenty of white space make this a comfortable large print puzzle book for seniors and for anyone with tired eyes or low vision. The music page beside each grid turns every puzzle into a short lesson in music history, so you finish each one knowing a little more about the composers, singers and stories behind the great operas.

A musical gift they will actually use

Looking for opera gifts for women and men, a present for a music lover, a thank-you gift for a music teacher or choir director, or a stocking stuffer for the musician who has every recording? This music activity book is a thoughtful, screen-free gift for opera fans, singers, retirees and grandparents, and a relaxing way to keep the mind active.
