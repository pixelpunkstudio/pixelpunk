About this book

Buzz, wiggle and flutter into the cutest bug adventure ever!

Cute Bug Days is a bug and insect coloring book for toddlers, preschoolers and kids ages 3 to 10 with 50 adorable bugs and insects, each one in its own natural home: a ladybug in the flower garden, a dragonfly over the pond, a firefly glowing at dusk, a scorpion under the desert stars and many more. Every page also has a "Did you know?" fun fact that little bug lovers will want to share with everyone.

What's inside
  • 50 unique coloring pages: 50 different bugs, never the same one twice
  • Single-sided pages: every drawing is printed on its own page, so markers won't ruin the next picture
  • A fun fact on every page, written in simple words for young kids
  • A story page beside every drawing to write or draw their own bug adventure
  • Bold, thick lines and big shapes, easy to color for toddlers, preschoolers and big kids, and great for building fine motor skills
  • Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages
Explore six bug worlds
  • The garden: ladybug, honeybee, snail, earthworm, garden spider, roly-poly and the praying mantis
  • The meadow: monarch butterfly, bumblebee, grasshopper and fireflies glowing at dusk
  • The pond and the stream: dragonfly, water strider, mayfly and the caddisfly larva in its pebble house
  • The forest: centipede, millipede, springtail and the polyphemus moth with its giant eye spots
  • The rainforest: leafcutter ant, leaf insect and the mighty rhinoceros beetle
  • The desert and the savanna: scorpion, antlion, termites and the dung beetle rolling its ball
Learning through coloring

Did you know that bees dance to talk, that katydids hear with their knees, or that some cicadas live underground for 17 years? Each fun fact turns coloring time into a little science lesson about nature and insects. Kids also learn that spiders and roly-polies are not insects at all, and meet tiny wonders like the water bear, which can survive freezing cold.

For parents, grandparents and teachers
  • A calm, screen-free bug activity book for home, road trips and rainy days
  • Story pages that spark imagination and early writing
  • Ready-made nature lessons for preschool, kindergarten, homeschool and classroom quiet time
A great gift

Perfect for birthdays, Easter baskets, Christmas stockings and summer vacations. A screen-free activity for curious kids who love nature, gardens and creepy crawlies.
